About

Native Wireless LLC is a software company in New Mexico, formed in 2021. We build small utilities that respect the person using them. Each app does a single job, works without an account, and never phones home. We charge one price because software we built once is not a service you should have to rent.

Privacy policy

Our apps do not collect your data. That is the whole policy, and here is what it means in plain terms.

  • No accounts. None of our apps ask you to sign up or log in.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind. We cannot see how you use the app, and we like it that way.
  • No servers of ours. Our apps have no backend. What you record, write, or generate stays on your device unless you share it (for example, exporting a PDF report and sending it to someone).
  • Payments are handled by Apple. Purchases go through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and we could not store them if we wanted to.

Each app's App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected." That label is accurate because there is nothing in the apps that could collect data. There is no account system, no analytics, and no server to send anything to.

Common questions

Why is there one price instead of a subscription?

Our apps are software we built once, not services we pay to run every month. So each app is free to download, lets you complete the job once for free and keep the result, and offers a single one-time purchase to unlock unlimited use. No subscription, nothing recurring, nothing to cancel.

I paid on my old phone. How do I get my purchase back?

Open the app's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they follow you to any new device signed into the same account, including family members, where Family Sharing is enabled.

How do refunds work?

Apple processes all App Store payments and refunds. We never see or handle your money. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple's process leaves you stuck, email us anyway and we will do what we can.

Where is my data stored?

On your device (see the privacy policy above). Deleting an app deletes its data, so export anything you want to keep first.
